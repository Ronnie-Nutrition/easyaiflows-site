Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

EasyAiFlows ("EasyAiFlows," "we," "us," or "our"), a sole proprietorship based in Pearland, Texas, operates easyaiflows.com (the "Site") and provides AI automation services and communications. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have — including for SMS/text messaging.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide — name, email address, phone number, and business details — through our forms, consultations, or when you contact us.
  • Communications you send us by text, email, or phone.
  • Usage data collected automatically through cookies and analytics/advertising tools (including the Meta Pixel) such as pages visited and device information.

SMS / Text Messaging

If you opt in, EasyAiFlows may send you SMS text messages for customer care, account and appointment notifications, and promotional offers. You opt in by providing your phone number and agreeing to receive texts on our website forms, in person or during a consultation, or by texting a keyword such as JOIN.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Mobile information sharing: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve our services, respond to inquiries, send the communications you opted in to receive, process bookings and notifications, and meet legal and compliance obligations.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (for example, hosting, scheduling, payment, and messaging providers) and only as needed to provide those services. As stated above, mobile opt-in and consent data is never shared with third parties for marketing.

Cookies & Advertising

Our Site uses cookies and the Meta Pixel to understand usage and measure advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You may opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link, opt out of texts by replying STOP, and request access to or deletion of your information by contacting us below.

Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent changes.
