Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI book cleaning appointments?

Yes. AI booking forms let clients select their service, home size, and schedule — then automatically adds the job to your calendar, sends a confirmation, and creates reminders.

How does AI help cleaning businesses get more reviews?

AI sends a review request text/email after every completed job with a direct link to your Google Business page. Timing it right after the clean (when the house looks great) gets the best results.
