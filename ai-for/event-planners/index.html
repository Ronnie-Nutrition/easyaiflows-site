Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI help event planners manage vendors?

AI automates vendor communication — sending confirmations, timeline updates, logistics details, and payment reminders on a schedule you set. It tracks responses and flags vendors who haven't confirmed.

Can AI manage RSVPs?

Yes. AI can send invitations, track RSVPs, send reminders to non-responders, manage dietary restrictions, and generate seating assignments — all automatically.

Will AI help me take on more events?

Absolutely. The biggest bottleneck for event planners is communication overhead. AI handles the routine half of it — vendor confirmations, timeline updates, client status — which is the part that scales badly when you add another event to the calendar.
