# EasyAiFlows — Ad Teleprompter Scripts

Scripts formatted for reading off a phone mounted near the camera.
One thought per line. Short lines = easy eye-scan. **BOLD** = emphasize.
[Brackets] = stage directions or pauses.

| Section | Time | Cumulative |
|---------|------|-----------|

### Performance Notes

- **Pause after "heart broken"** — let it create curiosity. Don't rush.
- **Slow down on the URL** — "easyaiflows" then "dot com" then "slash grader" — give viewers time to remember it
- **Hold up fingers** for the three questions — the visual reinforces the audio
- **Smile at the end** — the close should feel warm, not salesy

## RECORDING TIPS

**Setup (one-time):**
- Phone vertical for Reels/Stories, horizontal for Feed/Desktop
- Phone at eye level — NOT below (chin/nose angle is unflattering)
- Light on your face (window or ring light) — green wall behind you
- Sit close enough that your face fills the upper third of frame

**Per-take checklist:**
- Look at the **camera lens**, not the teleprompter text (glance, then look up)
- Record 2–3 takes — pick the best one in post
- After each take, watch on the phone before re-recording
- Smile at the start and end — even if you don't think you're smiling, audiences read it as warmth

**Before exporting:**
- Trim dead space (any silence > 1 second at start/end)
- Confirm audio is clean (no background noise, no echo)
- Export in highest quality your phone allows

## POST-PRODUCTION CHECKLIST

Before uploading to Meta Ads Manager:

- [ ] Burned-in captions added (CapCut, Descript, or Submagic)
- [ ] End card with URL: **easyaiflows.com/grader** (3-5 seconds)
- [ ] Two aspect ratios exported: 9:16 (vertical) AND 16:9 (horizontal)
- [ ] Audio normalized — no clipping, no quiet spots
- [ ] Hook visible/audible within first 3 seconds
- [ ] CTA clear in last 5 seconds

## URL VARIATIONS (test which converts best)

When saying the URL on camera, you can A/B these:

- **"easyaiflows.com/grader"** — clean, direct
- **"easyaiflows.com forward-slash grader"** — clearer pronunciation
- **"go to easyaiflows.com and click the grader link"** — softer, more natural

End-card text should always be: **easyaiflows.com/grader**
