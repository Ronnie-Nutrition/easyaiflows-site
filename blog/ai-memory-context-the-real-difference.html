Which AI Is the Best? The Real Answer Is Memory, Not the Model

By Ronnie Craig • June 30, 2026 • 6 min read

Everyone keeps asking the same question: "Which AI is the best?" People assume the impressive results they see are because of the specific AI someone's using. But here's the truth that changes everything once it clicks: we all have access to the same models. You can subscribe to the exact same frontier model the pros use for about $20 a month. The only difference in price is how much you can use it — not the quality.

The model isn't the moat. If the model were the whole story, everyone on the same subscription would get the same results — and they don't. The real differentiator is memory and context. For an AI, context is king.

Why two people on the identical AI get wildly different results

Picture two people both paying for the same top model. One types a one-line prompt with no background and gets generic, forgettable output. The other has fed the AI their notes, documents, brand voice, and project history — and gets sharp, tailored, almost-reads-your-mind results. Same model. Completely different outcomes. The difference is entirely the context each one provided.

The trap 99% of people are stuck in

Out of the box, most AI chat remembers very little — basically your current conversation and maybe a few scraps. That's it. So most people are running a brilliant engine with almost zero context about who they are or what they're doing. Then they conclude "AI is overhyped" — when really they just never gave it anything to work with. They're chasing a better model when the thing holding them back is memory.

The next step (it's smaller than you think)

The leap isn't a better model. It's giving your AI a real memory. Instead of only chatting in the app, you point the AI at a folder of your information — using something like Claude's Cowork or Claude Code — and load it up with what you know:

  • Notes on your projects, clients, and ideas
  • Your brand voice and guidelines
  • Standard operating procedures and past work
  • Anything you'd want a great assistant to already know

A lot of people keep this as simple markdown files — lightweight, easy to maintain, easy for the AI to search — a personal knowledge base the AI can draw on. The more organized and accessible that information is, the more the AI's output goes into overdrive. Suddenly it understands what you're doing, how you do it, and why.

The reframe: Stop asking "which AI is best?" and start asking "what context am I giving it?" A top model with rich context about your business beats the same model with no context, every single time.

Where to start

  1. Pick your AI — Claude, ChatGPT, whatever you like. They're close enough that it's not the bottleneck.
  2. Create a context folder — a few markdown files about your business, your voice, your projects.
  3. Point the AI at it — via Cowork or Claude Code — so it reads your real information instead of guessing.
  4. Add to it as you go. Every time you explain something to the AI, save it. Your context compounds, and so do your results.
