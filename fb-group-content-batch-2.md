## POST #18 — "Quick Tip / Hack" (Saturday, Week 2)

**Pillar:** Actionable
**Goal:** Bookmarkable, drives immediate use

> The "brain dump" method — my most-used AI workflow.
>
> When I need to write something (email, post, proposal, pitch) but can't figure out how to start, I do this:
>
> Open Claude. Type: "Here's my messy brain dump about [topic]. Clean this up into clear, professional copy in my voice — direct, no fluff, like I'm talking to a smart friend."
>
> Then I just type whatever's in my head. No grammar. No structure. Half-sentences. Random thoughts. Whatever.
>
> Claude organizes it, tightens it, and gives it back in a format I can actually send.
>
> I've written proposals this way. Client emails. This post (originally) was a brain dump.
>
> The goal isn't to let AI do your thinking. It's to stop letting the blank page slow you down.
>
> Try it this week. Tell me what you used it for. 👇
