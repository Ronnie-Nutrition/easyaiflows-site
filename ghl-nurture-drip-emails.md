Before I recommend any AI tool to anyone, I ask three questions. You can use these on yourself right now.

**Question 1: What tasks do you push off until "Monday"?**

I'm not talking about the big stuff. I mean the small, recurring things that you keep putting off — invoicing, social media posts, replying to inquiries. The things you'd happily pay $20/hour to never do again.

Write them down. Be honest.

**Question 2: If you had a personal assistant working 24 hours a day, what would they do first?**

This question reveals the highest-leverage thing AI could do for you. Most business owners answer something like:
- "Reply to leads in under 5 minutes"
- "Post on social media consistently"
- "Send invoices automatically"

That answer = your first AI workflow.

**Question 3: What's blocking you from doing it already?**

Almost always one of three things:

## Email #4 — Tactical Recommendation

**Send:** 9 days after Email #1

**Subject line:** Here's what I'd automate first if I were you

**Preview text:** Doesn't matter what business you're in...

---

I get asked this all the time: "Where should I START with AI?"

After helping dozens of business owners through it, my answer is almost always the same:

**Start with lead follow-up.**

Here's why:

Studies from Harvard Business Review and InsideSales.com confirm what every business owner already feels — **if you contact a lead within 5 minutes, you're 9× more likely to close than if you wait 30+ minutes.**

But most small businesses can't reply to leads in 5 minutes. You're at lunch. You're with a client. You're driving. The lead waits, gets cold, hires your competitor.

AI fixes this with a workflow that:

1. **Detects when a lead fills out your form** (web form, Facebook ad, Instagram DM)
2. **Sends them a personalized SMS within 30 seconds** ("Hey [Name], it's [you] — got your inquiry, what's a good time to chat?")
3. **Notifies your phone** so you can call them while they're still warm
4. **Books them on your calendar** if they reply with availability

I built this for my own business. Setup time: 2 hours. Cost: ~$30/month. Result: my close rate doubled.

You can build this yourself if you have time. Or we can build it together on a strategy call:

Even if you only have ONE lead a week, this workflow pays for itself instantly. If you have 10/week, it's the single highest-ROI AI move you can make.
