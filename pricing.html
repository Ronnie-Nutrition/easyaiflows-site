Pricing Questions

Straight Answers Before You Commit

How much does AI automation cost with EasyAiFlows?

Plans start at $97/month for a single custom AI assistant (Starter). Professional is $397/month for done-for-you agents, integrations, and custom workflows, and Enterprise is $997/month for a full automation suite. Every plan includes setup, training, and ongoing support — no hidden fees, cancel anytime.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. Every plan has a 30-day satisfaction guarantee — if you don't see the value in the first month, you get your money back. Most clients see results in the first week, and you can start with a free 15-minute strategy call before spending a dollar.

Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude myself?

You can — we use those tools daily too. The difference is that a custom AI agent takes action inside your business: logging into your email, drafting responses in your voice, booking appointments, and following up with leads automatically. ChatGPT is a tool; this is a system that runs on autopilot.

Do I need to be technical?

No. EasyAiFlows is built by a business owner, not a developer, specifically so you don't have to be technical. We handle setup, integrations, and ongoing optimization — you just tell us what's eating your time.

Is there a discount for paying annually?

Yes — pay annually and get about 15% off, which works out to roughly two months free: $990/year for Starter, $4,050/year for Professional, and $10,170/year for Enterprise.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Most clients start on Starter or Professional and upgrade as they see results. You can move up or down at any time — there are no long-term contracts.
